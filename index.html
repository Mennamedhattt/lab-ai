🎭 Sentiment Detective

Two tiny models, like the lab: a unigram model (each word alone) and a bigram model (also sees phrases such as “not good”). Type a review and compare!

Try “Not good at all”: the unigram model is fooled because “good” outweighs “not”, while the bigram model gets it right. Also try “Not a bad movie, I enjoyed it”.
